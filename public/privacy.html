What we collect, and why

  • Your account: your email address, and your password (stored only as a salted, one-way hash). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and an identifier for your Apple or Google account, nothing else. Used to run your account and sign you in.
  • Your progress: the name and emblem you choose, your decks, campaign, settings, cards, currencies, level, rank and game record. Used to provide the game and keep your progress on any device.
  • Games online: the moves of games you play online, while they last, and the results of ranked games. Used to run online and ranked play.
  • Anonymous game statistics: when a game ends, a summary of it: the cards in each deck, which cards were played and when, who went first, who won and how long it took, the game's version and the day. It holds nothing about who played: no name, account, email, device or address, and it can't be linked back to you. Used to balance the game (to see how decks and cards do against each other). You can switch it off in the game's options ("share game stats"); online games are summarised by our server the same way.
  • Decks you share: only if you choose to share a deck with the community: its name, its cards, the note you add, and the name you go by in the game, shown with it as the credit. Anyone signed in can see it and save a copy. We keep which account shared it, so only you can take it down, and a fingerprint of each account that saved it (not the account itself), so each player counts once towards how often it was saved. Deck codes you copy and send yourself never reach us.
  • Security: your IP address, briefly, to limit repeated sign-in attempts; and a record of when you agreed to these documents.

The legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running your account and the game), our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and fair, and our legal obligations.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in, and your device's storage to keep a copy of your progress and settings. Both are needed for the game to work; we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the game and its database.
  • Resend, which sends password-reset emails (your email address only).
  • Apple or Google, if you choose to sign in with them.

We don't sell your data, or share it for advertising. Some of these providers may process data outside your country, under appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account (from your profile in the game), your account and progress are deleted straight away; records of past ranked games no longer link to you. Sign-in attempt records last 15 minutes; reset links an hour. Anonymous game statistics aren't personal data and aren't linked to your account, so they stay after it is deleted. Decks you shared are taken down when you delete your account (or whenever you take one down yourself); copies other players saved are theirs.
